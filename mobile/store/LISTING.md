# Maaji Restaurant — store listing texts

Paste-ready texts for App Store Connect and Google Play Console, German
(primary language) and English. Every field sits in its own block; the
limit in each heading is the store's, and check them with
`python3 mobile/store/check_listing.py` (counts characters per block).

Written 2026-11-24 from what the app actually does (Release build against
maaji-freiburg.com). Deliberately **not** claimed: diet *filters* (the app
shows diet labels, it does not filter), *live* tracking (it shows the order
status), fixed point values or the gift-card discount (both are set by the
restaurant and can change). Please read it in your own voice before
pasting — it is your restaurant.

• Stimmt etwas mit Ihrer Bestellung nicht? Melden Sie es über „Reklamationen“, gern mit Foto
• Konto jederzeit in der App löschen

Die App gibt es auf Deutsch, Englisch, Französisch, Italienisch, Spanisch und Arabisch.

Maaji – indisch-pakistanische Leckereien in Freiburg. Mit Liebe serviert.

## Before you paste — three things to confirm

1. **Google sign-in** is not in the Android build (no Google client IDs at
   build time), so the Play texts say "Anmelden mit E-Mail" / "Sign in
   with email". If you switch Google sign-in on later, add it back.
2. **Delivery** — the copy says "in unser Liefergebiet in Freiburg". If you
   deliver beyond Freiburg, name the area.
3. **Dish list** — "Suppen, Chat-Bowls, Currys, Biryani, Wraps, Naan" matches
   the menu today. Adjust it if the menu changes before release.
